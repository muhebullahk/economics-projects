## Suggested Processing Order

The papers cluster into three thematic groups. I suggest reading them in this order to build cumulative conceptual scaffolding:

### Group A — Measurement Foundations (read first)
1. Davis & Haltiwanger (1992) — establishes gross job flow methodology
2. Davis & Haltiwanger (1999) — structural VAR on driving forces
3. Hopenhayn (1992) — canonical theory of firm entry/exit dynamics

### Group B — Business Cycle Labour Flows (read second)
4. Campbell & Fisher (2000) — adjustment cost model of job creation/destruction asymmetry
5. Lazear & Spletzer (2012) — churn decomposition, Great Recession
6. Hyatt & McEntarfer (2012) — job-to-job flows, Great Recession
7. Mueller (2017) — worker sorting into unemployment over the cycle
8. Krusell et al. (2017) — gross worker flows model (E/U/N)
9. Carrillo-Tudela & Visschers (2023) — occupational reallocation and unemployment duration

### Group C — Firm Dynamics and Growth (read third)
10. Asplund & Nocke (2006) — firm turnover in imperfectly competitive markets
11. Acemoglu et al. (2018) — innovation, reallocation, and industrial policy
12. Hopenhayn et al. (2022) — population growth, firm demographics, concentration
